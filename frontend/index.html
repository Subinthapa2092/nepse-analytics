Not included from the plugin pack, on purpose: heatmap-series, box-whisker, stacked-area/bars, grouped-bars, dual-range-histogram, lollipop, brushable-area, background-shade, overlay-price-scale, pretty-histogram, partial-price-line, accessibility helper. These are built for multi-series comparisons or statistical distributions (e.g. comparing many symbols at once, OHLC distributions across a portfolio) — they don't have a natural fit on a single-symbol candlestick + volume dashboard, so adding them would clutter this view rather than improve it. Rounded-candles-series was also left out: it requires writing a full custom series renderer from scratch, which is real risk-of-bugs work I didn't want to rush. Ask if you want any of these anyway and I'll build them.
